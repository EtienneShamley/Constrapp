# Constrapp — Product Overview

## What It Is

**Constrapp is the connected commercial operating system for construction projects.**

Australian-built and web-first, it gives builders, contractors, and quantity surveyors one connected commercial dataset that runs from preconstruction through to final account. It replaces spreadsheets and disconnected tools with a single source of commercial truth covering budgets, procurement, purchase orders, variations, progress claims, supplier invoices, forecasting, and margin — with **Cost Codes as the spine** that joins every stage.

This document describes both what is **implemented today** and the **product intent**. Each module below is marked accordingly — do not read an unmarked ambition as shipped behaviour. Technical status detail lives in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) and [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).

## The Connected Commercial Lifecycle

Constrapp models one continuous commercial dataset, not a set of disconnected tools. The same data flows through every stage:

```
Drawing → Quantity → BOQ → Estimate → Tender → Award → Approved Budget
  → Commitment → Purchase Order → Variation → Progress Claim
  → Supplier Invoice → Actual Cost → Forecast → Cash Flow
  → Final Project Margin → Final Account
```

Field information exists to serve a commercial outcome — never as an end in itself:

- Site progress → forecast update
- Approved variation → budget and commitment update
- Defect or delay → project risk and forecast impact
- Drawing measurement → BOQ quantity
- Subcontractor bid → award and commitment
- Site photo → progress or claim evidence

## Cost Codes — The Commercial Spine

Cost Codes are the single join key connecting every commercial stage. A cost code links a BOQ line to an estimate, an estimate to a tender package, an award to a budget line, a budget line to a purchase order, and a PO to its variations, claims, and supplier invoices. Every commercial document references a cost code and snapshots its display name at write time, so the whole lifecycle reconciles through one taxonomy. New commercial modules must integrate through the cost-code spine rather than operate independently.

## Target Market

- Small-to-medium Australian construction companies (primary)
- Contractors in other markets (NZ, ZA, GB, US, Eurozone) can report in their own **currency**; note that **tax calculation remains Australian GST** until a country-specific tax foundation ships, so Constrapp is not yet tax-compliant outside Australia
- Project managers, quantity surveyors, company admins
- Subcontractors and clients who need controlled read/limited access

## User Roles

Product intent — the full role model the product is designed around:

| Role | Intended Access |
|---|---|
| Super Admin | Platform owner — cross-company visibility |
| Company Admin | Full access within their company |
| Project Manager | Projects, budgets, contacts, drawings, POs, claims |
| QS / Office | BOQ, budgets, forecasting, claims assessment, reports |
| Subcontractor | Assigned projects, drawings, POs, photos |
| Client | Dashboard, pulse view, photos, reports |

**Implemented today:** role is a field on the user's Firestore document; security rules distinguish only *company member (read)* vs *company_admin / project_manager / qs (write)*. Super Admin has no special powers yet, and Subcontractor/Client scoping is not implemented. See [docs/SECURITY.md](docs/SECURITY.md).

## Commercial Workflow Module Tiers

Status legend: **Implemented** (foundation shipped) · **Partial** (some functionality, gaps noted) · **Placeholder** (screen exists, no functionality) · **Future** (not built). Modules are grouped by their role in the commercial lifecycle, not by screen.

### Tier 1 — Commercial spine (the connected lifecycle)

- **Cost Codes** — *Implemented (foundation).* Company-wide taxonomy (code, name, category, unit) reused across all projects; the join key for every commercial stage.
- **Approved Budget (Budgets)** — *Implemented (foundation).* Per-project budget lines against company-wide cost codes, tracking **Budgeted, Committed, Claimed, Actual, Invoiced, Remaining**. All six are derived live from POs, progress claims, and supplier invoices — none are stored on budget lines. Committed is *remaining open commitment* — PO value net of posted invoicing **and** of approved claims already counted in Actual (ADR-55), so a certified claim is never in both figures and Forecast Final Cost counts it once. The page shows *Unavailable*, never $0, when a source read fails, and flags commitment still open on closed POs. Definitions: [docs/FINANCIAL_WORKFLOWS.md](docs/FINANCIAL_WORKFLOWS.md).
- **Purchase Orders (Commitment)** — *Implemented (foundation).* Create draft POs with embedded line items against cost codes, transactional PO numbering, draft → sent → closed/cancelled lifecycle. Suppliers are picked from company Contacts (with inline quick-create); sending to suppliers by email/PDF is future.
- **Progress Claims** — *Implemented (foundation).* Cumulative supplier claims against sent POs: claimed-to-date entry per PO line, overclaim warnings, one open claim per PO, assessment with per-line certification, partial approval, retention and GST handling.
- **Supplier Invoices (Actual Cost)** — *Implemented (foundation).* Accounts-payable supplier bills (`SI-0001`), created either directly against a sent/closed PO (`direct_po`) or from one approved progress claim (`progress_claim`). Per-line ex-GST amounts with per-line tax codes (GST / GST-free / input-taxed), retention carried from the claim, `draft → approved → posted` lifecycle (posted is immutable). Posted/paid invoices drive **Invoiced**, mature **Committed** to remaining open commitment, and feed **Actual** (replacing their source claim without double-counting). Reads restricted to internal financial roles. **Payments are now implemented** (see Supplier Payments below) — but note that the reserved `paid` status and `paidAt` field were deliberately **deprecated in place rather than activated**: payment state is derived from payment allocations, never authored on the invoice. **Supplier Credit Notes are now implemented** (see Credit Notes below) — a posted, retention-free invoice can receive one, which reduces its Invoiced/Actual contribution and remaining payable at read time without ever mutating it; the invoice's own reserved `docType: 'credit_note'` / `adjustsInvoiceId` fields are superseded and stay unactivated. Attachments and accounting sync remain reserved/future.
- **Client Invoices / Accounts Receivable** — *Implemented (foundation).* The revenue-side register (`CI-0001`), answering what has been invoiced to the client, what remains available to invoice, when payment was due, and which approved Client Variations have been billed. Invoices are controlled against the **Current Contract Sum** (`Available to Invoice = Current Contract Sum − Issued Client Invoices`, ex-GST) and may carry **contract lines** and **approved Client Variation lines** on one document, with per-variation invoiced/remaining balances that prevent double-invoicing. Lifecycle `draft → issued → void` — issued invoices are immutable and void is terminal, and **both are enforced by Firestore rules**, the first collection in the app where they are. Client identity and payment terms are snapshotted; due dates are suggested from the client's terms with the source named, and left blank when no terms exist. Over-invoicing is **warned with an explicit acknowledgement, never blocked**. **Receipts now settle these invoices** (see Client Receipts below): **Received to Date**, **Remaining to Reconcile**, and a derived reconciliation state (*Unreconciled · Partly · Fully · Over-reconciled*) are shown per invoice, and ageing now ages the **remaining balance**. There is still **no `paid` status on the invoice** — payment state is derived from receipt allocations, never authored. **⚠️ Not a Tax Invoice** — company legal name, ABN, and address are not captured, so there is no printable invoice, PDF, email, or "Tax Invoice" labelling; an optional External Invoice Reference ties each record to the invoice actually issued from your accounting system. Reads restricted to internal financial roles. Definitions: [docs/FINANCIAL_WORKFLOWS.md](docs/FINANCIAL_WORKFLOWS.md).
- **Client Receipts (Cash Received)** — *Implemented (foundation).* The settlement half of accounts receivable, and Constrapp's first real cash record (`CR-0001`). A receipt records **gross cash actually received** from a client — date, amount, payment method, bank and external references — with **embedded allocations** against issued Client Invoices. Lifecycle `draft → posted → void` (void terminal, non-empty reason required), **rules-enforced** like Client Invoices, with posted receipts immutable. One receipt may settle several invoices and several receipts may settle one; receipts may be left **partly or entirely unallocated** ("money on account"), which reduces no invoice balance and is never auto-applied — an explicit *Allocate oldest first* action produces an editable proposal. **Received to Date, Remaining to Reconcile, reconciliation state, and the corrected AR ageing are all derived at read time and never written onto an invoice**, so voiding a receipt restores every balance with no reversal record. Over-allocating an invoice is **warned with an explicit acknowledgement, never blocked**; over-allocating the receipt itself is hard-blocked. **⚠️ Cash is not revenue** — a receipt carries no GST, no net amount, and no tax code, and feeds no budget, forecast, or margin figure. A **future-dated** receipt can be drafted but not posted. Reads restricted to internal financial roles. Definitions: [docs/FINANCIAL_WORKFLOWS.md](docs/FINANCIAL_WORKFLOWS.md).
- **Supplier Payments (Cash Paid)** — *Implemented (foundation).* The settlement half of accounts payable and the money-out mirror of Client Receipts (`SP-0001`). A payment records **gross cash actually paid** to a supplier or subcontractor — date, amount, payment method, bank, remittance and external references — with **embedded allocations** against **posted** Supplier Invoices. Lifecycle `draft → posted → void` (void terminal, non-whitespace reason required), **rules-enforced**, with posted payments immutable. **Allocations settle each invoice's `payableTotal`, never its `grossTotal`** — retention withheld is not payable, and no payment ever writes, clears, or reduces a retention field (retention release remains unmodelled). One payment may settle several invoices and several payments may settle one; payments may be left **partly or entirely unallocated** ("money on account"), which reduces no invoice balance and is never auto-applied — but whose **full amount is still actual Cash Out**. **Paid to Date, Remaining Payable, reconciliation state, and AP ageing on the remaining balance are all derived at read time and never written onto a supplier invoice**, so voiding a payment restores every balance with no reversal, refund, or bank-reversal record. Over-reconciling an invoice is **warned with an explicit acknowledgement, never blocked**; over-allocating the payment itself is hard-blocked. **⚠️ Cash out is not cost** — a payment settles an Actual cost the posted invoice already recognised, so it carries no GST and feeds no budget, forecast, or margin figure. **⚠️ The supplier-invoice `paid` status and `paidAt` field are DEPRECATED IN PLACE, never activated** — payment state has one source of truth. A **future-dated** payment can be drafted but not posted. Reads restricted to internal financial roles. **The Actual Cash Flow foundation now reads these payments** (see Cash Flow below). Definitions: [docs/FINANCIAL_WORKFLOWS.md](docs/FINANCIAL_WORKFLOWS.md).
- **BOQ & Estimating** — *BOQ implemented (foundation); Estimating planned.* The project's measured **Bill of Quantities** against cost codes (ADR-32 Part 1): items carry an optional user item number and section, a description, quantity, unit (prefilled from the cost code, editable), a **mandatory cost code**, and — once priced — an ex-GST rate with a **derived** amount (`quantity × rate`, whole-cent, **rules-enforced**). **A blank rate means unpriced** (`null`, never 0): unpriced items count toward nothing and **suppress** the BOQ-vs-budget variance until priced. Lifecycle `active → void` (terminal, reasoned), rules-enforced; no delete. **The BOQ feeds no financial figure** — its only derived output is the read-time **BOQ vs Approved Budget** comparison by cost code, on the BOQ tab. Reads restricted to internal financial roles (the BOQ is the internal estimate). *Deferred:* estimating (margin/overheads), **BOQ → Budget transfer**, multiple/versioned BOQs, attachments, takeoff linkage — see [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).
- **Tender & Award** — *Implemented (foundation).* The step between estimate and commitment, deliberately **BOQ-independent** (cost-code + free-text scope; an optional BOQ scope schedule is a separate future step): **Tender Packages** (`TP-0001`) scope work as **≥1 selected cost codes + free-text scope** with a `draft → issued → awarded / cancelled` lifecycle (issuing freezes the scope; only the closing date and notes stay editable; the **closing date is informational only — late bids are never blocked**). **Tender Bids** are manually transcribed from supplier/subcontractor contacts and priced **per cost code** ex-GST (no GST fields) with a `received → void` lifecycle; bids freeze permanently when the package is awarded or cancelled (rules-enforced). **No totals are stored anywhere** — no `bidTotal`, no `awardTotal`: every figure is derived at read time through a validity gate that flags malformed bids and excludes them from comparison and award instead of trusting them. The read-time **Tender Comparison** (never "bid levelling") shows each bid's derived total, **variance to Approved Budget (Approved Budget − Bid; unavailable — never zero — when no budget lines exist)**, variance to lowest, exclusions, and a per-cost-code matrix. The **award is a commercial decision record only** — winning bid, bidder snapshot, decision notes — rules-verified (same package, received bid, single award) and **creating no Purchase Order and changing no budget, commitment, actual, forecast, margin, or cash-flow figure**; the Awarded Bid Value is displayed on the Tender pages only and never netted against POs. QS may award. Reads restricted to internal financial roles — a bid is competitor pricing. **"Raise purchase order" from an award — *live since 29 October 2026 (M3-A, ADR-61)*:** on an awarded package, one reviewed, explicitly confirmed action creates a **draft** purchase order from the winning bid — the supplier and every cost-coded ex-GST amount carried across (one PO line per bid line; subtotal equals the awarded value; GST added at commitment), the bid's exclusions shown for reading and never silently bound — linked both ways so one award raises one current PO, with *Raise again* only after that PO is cancelled. The draft is reviewed and sent through the normal Purchase Orders workflow; **nothing is sent automatically and nothing is committed until it is sent.** *Deferred:* BOQ scope schedules, invitations, bidder portal, clarifications/alternates, revisions, bid levelling, approval workflow, award rescission, and splitting an award across several POs. Definitions: [docs/FINANCIAL_WORKFLOWS.md](docs/FINANCIAL_WORKFLOWS.md); rationale: ADR-32 Part 2, ADR-61.
- **Variations** — *Implemented (foundation).* One type-discriminated collection: **Client Variations** (`CV-0001`, head-contract revenue changes) and **Supplier Variations** (`SV-0001`, subcontract commitment changes; one PO or none). Cost-code spine on every line, per-line tax codes (ex-GST canonical, negatives supported), `draft → submitted → approved`/`rejected`/`withdrawn` lifecycle with unbounded per-line approval. Approved-only, read-time: approved supplier variations surface as **Commitment Exposure** (separate from Committed) on the Budget tab; approved client variations are revenue-side only. No Budget Line/PO/claim/invoice mutation. **M3-B1 (ADR-62) — implemented, NOT YET DEPLOYED:** an approved supplier variation that names a sent/closed PO is **applied to that PO's commitment** — approval writes rules-stamped `commitmentAppliedAt/By`; variations approved before M3-B1 stay unapplied until an explicit one-time *Apply to commitment* — and its existing-line amounts revise the PO line value inside Remaining Committed (so inside Forecast Final Cost, once), matured by the same claims and invoices; new-scope lines stay as labelled unapplied exposure (awaiting M3-B2); the PO document is never modified; a CTC-overlap warning and acknowledgement guard the Forecast input. *Deferred (M3-B2):* claim/invoice-against-variation linkage for new-scope lines (the reserved `variationId`), and internal Budget Adjustments (a separate future type).
- **Forecast Cost to Complete** — *Implemented (foundation).* A forward-looking, **strictly cost-side** control layer answering "what do we currently expect this project to cost when complete?" One manual input per cost code — **Uncommitted Cost to Complete** (`number | null`; `null` = not forecast, `0` = reviewed/no further cost) — stored in per-cost-code `forecastLines` keyed by `costCodeId`; everything else is derived at read time. **Forecast Final Cost** = Actual + Remaining Committed + Uncommitted CTC; **Variance to Budget** = Budgeted − Forecast Final Cost. No automatic remaining-budget default (a Remaining Budget Reference backs an explicit "Use remaining budget" action). Approved and pending **supplier variation exposure are shown separately and never added** to the forecast (they do not yet mature against claims/invoices). Financial-role-only reads. Definitions: [docs/FINANCIAL_WORKFLOWS.md](docs/FINANCIAL_WORKFLOWS.md).
- **Project Margin** — *Implemented (foundation).* The first revenue-and-margin layer. A per-project **Commercial Baseline** stores the only authored inputs (Original Contract Value, Original Approved Budget, contract dates, client snapshot, notes); everything else is derived at read time (`lib/margin.js`): **Current Contract Sum** = Original Contract Value + Approved Client Variations, **Forecast Revenue** = Current Contract Sum, **Forecast Gross Profit** = Forecast Revenue − Forecast Final Cost, plus **Forecast Margin %**, **Original Planned Profit/Margin %**, and **Margin Movement**. All ex-GST. Pending client variations are separate revenue exposure; approved/pending supplier variations are separate cost exposure and are never added to Forecast Final Cost. Lives on the **Commercial** tab (Margin sub-view) with financial-role-only margin cards on Overview; reads restricted to internal financial roles. Cash flow and payments are **not** included, and margin is deliberately **unaffected** by invoicing — Client Invoices (since shipped, see above) report invoiced value alongside margin, never into it. Definitions: [docs/FINANCIAL_WORKFLOWS.md](docs/FINANCIAL_WORKFLOWS.md).
- **Company Country & Currency** — *Implemented (foundation).* Constrapp is no longer hard-coded to AUD. A company records its **country** (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2) and **base currency** (ISO 4217) on a Company Settings page (`company_admin` only): country *suggests* a currency, the admin *confirms or overrides* it, and every existing project is listed and pinned to an explicit currency in the same action — **no amount is ever converted**. New projects inherit the company base currency (overridable at creation); each project reports in **one** currency, which **locks** once the project holds any monetary value. Every financial screen displays the project currency through one shared formatter. **No FX conversion, no exchange rates, no mixed-currency transactions** — a currency is a label for amounts entered in it, never a conversion. **⚠️ Currency display is configurable; tax calculation is not** — GST remains a flat Australian 10% and every "GST 10%" label is Australian, so selecting NZ, ZA, US, or GB does **not** make Constrapp tax-compliant there. Country-specific tax configuration is a separate future foundation (see [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md)).
- **Cash Flow** — *Implemented (foundation) — actual, forecast, and visualisation.* The first Cash Flow output, deliberately **actual-only**: a **Cash Flow** sub-view on the Commercial tab showing **recorded cash movement** — Actual Cash In from posted Client Receipts (by `receiptDate`), Actual Cash Out from posted Supplier Payments (by `paymentDate`), monthly actual net, and a **cumulative position starting from zero** with dense monthly rows (gap months render as zero rows). Cash is always the **total** transaction amount (an unallocated advance or overpayment is still cash that moved — `allocatedTotal` is never the cash figure), grouped by the transaction date (never a created/posted timestamp), gross (inc. GST), and never summed across currencies. Unallocated cash in/out is reported separately as *on account*, never netted. A separate, clearly-labelled **accrual/ex-GST commercial context** panel shows Current Contract Sum, Forecast Revenue, Forecast Final Cost, Forecast Gross Profit, and Forecast Margin % without ever mixing them into a cash figure. **⚠️ Not a bank balance** — Constrapp models no bank account, opening cash position, financing, or GST/BAS remittance, and the page states this permanently. **Forecast Cash Flow has since shipped** on top of that foundation. Two further layers project forward: **open invoice balances by due date** — positive remaining on issued Client Invoices (gross) and on posted Supplier Invoices (`payableTotal`, net of retention) — and **manual monthly timing** of longer-term revenue and cost, authored as `cashFlowLines`. The load-bearing rule is that **months before the current month are actual-only**, so no forecast amount ever lands in a past month and nothing can be counted twice; past-due and undated invoice balances are **never guessed into a month** but reported as untimed. Manual lines carry an expected **gross** cash amount plus, separately, the **ex-GST source value** they represent (completeness only, never a cash column), and may time remaining uninvoiced contract value, remaining committed cost, uncommitted cost to complete, approved claims awaiting an invoice, or a free-form adjustment — **never an invoice balance**, which is already timed automatically. Outputs add a **projected cumulative position and closing position** (still from zero), revenue and cost **completeness** (shown as "—" rather than a false 0% or 100% when a basis is unavailable), **untimed reporting on three separate bases** (gross cash · ex-GST source value · informational exposure, never summed together), **stale-line handling** (a line whose month has passed stops counting and must be retimed forward or voided — never silently moved or deleted), and a **peak funding requirement** that is **suppressed** whenever significant amounts remain untimed. **⚠️ Retention release and GST/BAS cash movement are excluded and prominently warned about, and unallocated cash is flagged as a possible overstatement — none of these silently adjusts a figure.** Reads restricted to internal financial roles; timing lines are `active → void` (never deleted) with a rules-enforced lifecycle. **Cash Flow visualisation has since shipped**: a two-panel chart above the monthly table — monthly Cash In and Cash Out as diverging stacked bars around zero (**hue for direction, texture for actual vs forecast**, so the distinction never depends on colour alone), and the cumulative position from zero with an emphatic zero line and a shaded negative region. It is **presentation only** — it consumes the figures already derived above it and re-derives nothing, and the monthly table remains the exact numeric record. Its honesty rules match the table's: a past or unavailable forecast is shown as **unavailable, never as $0**, the cumulative line **breaks** rather than bridging a period it cannot publish, and the **peak-funding marker is drawn only when that figure is authoritative** — when peak funding is suppressed the chart shows **no marker at all** rather than plotting the lower bound. *Planned:* **date filtering** and **invoice retiming** — see [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md). Definitions: [docs/FINANCIAL_WORKFLOWS.md](docs/FINANCIAL_WORKFLOWS.md).
- **Final Account** — *Future.* Reconciliation of approved budget, variations, and actual cost into final project margin. *Planned.*

### Tier 2 — Commercial enablers

- **Projects** — *Implemented (foundation).* Create and list projects with status, budget, progress, and an inherited **currency**; Project Detail area with tabbed modules. No general edit/delete yet — currency is the one field editable after creation, and only until the project holds monetary data.
- **Contacts** — *Implemented (foundation).* Company-wide directory of suppliers, subcontractors, consultants, and clients (organisations and individuals) with ABN validation, trades, payment terms, GST status, embedded contact people, duplicate warnings, and archive/reactivate. Contacts can be assigned to any number of projects (administrative tags — contacts always stay company-wide); the PO supplier picker lists the current project's contacts first, and quick-created suppliers are auto-assigned to that project. New Purchase Orders pick their supplier from Contacts (`supplierId` + `supplierName` snapshot). Directory reads are restricted to internal financial roles.
- **Subcontractors** — *Partial.* Live filtered view of Contacts (type = subcontractor). IQ™ accountability scoring and budget vs cost code breakdown remain future.
- **Manual QS Takeoff** — *Implemented as Constrapp Quant™ (1A to 1D live in production — see Tier 4).* Measured quantities reach BOQ quantity lines by cost code through an explicit, confirmed application — the manual pipeline that Quant™ AI later accelerates.
- **Supplier Retention & Retention Release** — *Implemented (foundation).* The retention register and the release workflow, closing the one-way trapdoor that retention used to be: before this, a payment could only settle an invoice's payable, which is permanently net of retention, so retained money had **no route to ever becoming payable**. A **Retention** sub-view on the Commercial tab shows retention **held**, **released to date**, and **total withheld**, grouped by supplier and expandable to each posted supplier invoice. A **Retention Release** (`RR-0001`) is the new authored event that makes withheld retention payable — an **internal commercial authorisation**, explicitly **not** a supplier invoice, tax invoice, credit note, or payment, carrying no cost code and moving no cash by itself. Once posted, the released amount becomes part of that invoice's payable and is settled by an **ordinary Supplier Payment** — the payment module is otherwise unchanged. **Partial releases** are supported, and their GST is the **cumulative rounding delta** rather than each release rounding its own share, so any number of partials sums **exactly** to the invoice's stored retention GST and total. **Nothing is written onto a supplier invoice** — its retention figures are immutable for the life of the document — so voiding a release restores every balance at the next render with no reversal, credit note, or adjustment record. Lifecycle `draft → posted → void` (void terminal, reason required), **rules-enforced**, with the target invoice, the per-release cap, and the exact GST formula also enforced server-side. **⚠️ Releasing more than the retention available is hard-blocked in the app**, but Firestore rules cannot sum sibling releases, so concurrent releases remain possible and any over-release is **reported, never hidden**. **⚠️ Retention *paid* is deliberately not reported** — a payment settles an invoice balance as one balance, and nothing identifies which part it settled. Cost, forecast, and margin are untouched: a release moves cash, not cost. *Deferred:* client retention, retention due dates and defects-liability dates, contract-level retention percentages and caps, first-half/second-half semantics as a first-class concept, retention credit notes, and final-account linkage. Definitions: [docs/FINANCIAL_WORKFLOWS.md](docs/FINANCIAL_WORKFLOWS.md).
- **Payments and Receipts** — *Implemented (foundation).* **Both cash directions now exist** (see Tier 1 above): **Client Receipts** (cash in, allocated against issued client invoices) and **Supplier Payments** (cash out, allocated against posted supplier invoices' net payable). Both reuse one shared, direction-agnostic allocation foundation, and every balance is derived at read time — nothing is written onto an invoice in either direction. The supplier-invoice `paid` status and `paidAt` field are **deprecated in place, not activated**, so payment state never has two sources of truth. Retention release, refunds, payment reversals, payment runs, remittance output, and bank reconciliation remain unmodelled (supplier credit notes have since shipped — see Credit Notes below). Accounting integrations (Xero, MYOB, QuickBooks) attach via the `externalRefs` fields on POs, claims, invoices, receipts, and payments.
- **Credit Notes** — *Implemented (foundation) — supplier side.* **Supplier Credit Notes** (`SCN-0001`) record the reduction a supplier issued against **exactly one posted supplier invoice** — over-claimed quantities, rejected work, back-charges, negotiated reductions — in their own `supplierCreditNotes` collection (ADR-31). Cost-coded ex-GST lines with per-line tax codes (cost codes restricted to those the invoice charged), `draft → posted → void` lifecycle **rules-enforced**, and — a first — the rules **`get()` the target invoice**: it must exist, be posted, carry **zero retention**, match the credit's supplier and currency, and its payable must cover the credit's gross. Posted valid-target credits **reduce Invoiced and Actual by cost code** (flowing into Forecast Final Cost and Margin) and **reduce the invoice's Remaining Payable** (flowing into AP ageing, the payment picker, and Forecast Cash Out) — all at read time; the invoice document is never touched, so voiding a credit restores everything with no reversal record. Over-crediting beyond the payable is **hard-blocked** (cumulative cap app-enforced — rules cannot sum siblings, Deferred Control 25). Credit after full payment surfaces honestly as **money recoverable from the supplier** — no refund transaction is invented. Remaining Committed is deliberately **not** re-opened by a credit. Lives inside the **Supplier Invoices** view. **No** client credit notes, retained-invoice credits, refunds, attachments, or approval workflow. Definitions: [docs/FINANCIAL_WORKFLOWS.md](docs/FINANCIAL_WORKFLOWS.md).
- **Commercial Reporting** — *Placeholder.* Margin, cost-to-complete, cash-flow, and final-account reporting (not a generic export builder). *Planned.*

### Tier 3 — Field features (repositioned as commercial inputs/outputs)

Each field feature earns its place by feeding or evidencing a commercial outcome; none is a standalone reporting product.

- **Drawings & Documents** — *Implemented (foundation).* Controlled document register on the **Documents** project tab: a **Drawings** register of drawing masters with immutable revisions (`current` / `superseded` / `withdrawn`), full revision history, and an explicit succession decision on withdrawal; plus a flat **General Documents** register (specifications, contracts, certificates, safety, correspondence) with `project` / `internal` visibility. PDF/PNG/JPEG only; bytes in Firebase Storage, metadata in Firestore, uploads Storage-first. Drawing **reads are open to every provisioned company member** — including subcontractor and client — because a drawing is site information; drawing **writes** are company_admin/project_manager only (QS excluded), while general-document writes include QS. Nothing is ever hard-deleted. The immutable `{ drawingId, revisionId }` pair is the identity Quant™ takeoff records reference (ADR-51). **Not included:** photos, DWG, markup/annotation, OCR, AI, drawing comparison, thumbnails. See ADR-28.
- **Site Photos** — *Placeholder.* Progress and claim evidence: site photo → progress or claim evidence.
- **Project Timeline (Programme)** — *Implemented (foundation).* A practical construction programme, not a critical-path scheduling suite. Activities and **milestones** (a milestone is a flag on an activity: a single dated point, zero-day duration) with planned start/finish, optional actual start/finish, a five-state lifecycle (`not_started · in_progress · on_hold · completed · cancelled`), **manually entered** progress, an optional responsible **Contact**, and an optional **cost code** — the commercial-spine link. Overdue, days late, the look-ahead horizon (Overdue · This week · Upcoming · Later · Completed/Cancelled) and the four summary counts are **derived at read time and never stored**. Desktop shows summary cards, a **read-only Gantt** (calendar-day grid, today line, milestone diamonds, progress fill — built on CSS/SVG, **no new dependency**) and the full activity table, which remains the exact record; **mobile does not render the Gantt at all** and uses grouped activity cards instead. Reads are limited to `company_admin`, `project_manager` and **`qs`**; **only `company_admin` and `project_manager` can author** — the one place QS is read-only. Subcontractor and client access is **denied**, because those roles are not yet scoped to their own projects. Activities are **cancelled, never deleted** (terminal, reason required). **⚠️ This is a current-plan programme, not approved-baseline variance** — Constrapp stores no baseline, so it reports "late against the current plan" and never claims slippage against an approved programme, and the page says so. **⚠️ Progress is unverified and financially inert** — it is never derived from dates or Progress Claims and changes no budget, forecast, margin or cash figure; `projects/{projectId}.progress` is untouched. **Timeline & Scheduling Enhancements (ADR-46):** a **Due** filter (overdue only / due within 14 days), **quick status actions** for company admins and project managers (Start today, Put on hold, Complete today, milestone Mark reached today — they record only today's date and refuse to overwrite someone else's newer change; anything needing an earlier date goes through the full editor), the overdue state now rolls over at local midnight without a reload, and the Dashboard Programme panel shows the **next milestone**. This extends the programme; it is not a separate Tasks module. *Not included:* dependencies, critical path, automatic rescheduling, WBS/hierarchy, baselines, working calendars and public holidays, drag-to-reschedule, calendar view, print/PDF export, MS Project/Primavera import-export, notifications, and any automatic Forecast/Cash Flow effect — `delay → forecast impact` remains a **future read-time** derivation over the cost-code link, never a duplicated authored value.
- **RFIs — Requests for Information** — *Implemented (foundation).* The formal record of a question that needed clarification: who asked what, of whom, against which **drawing revision** or **document**, when it was due, what the answer was and when it arrived. **RFI V1 is an evidence layer for future delay / EOT / variation / forecast analysis** — it stores the record and stable commercial join keys only (an optional cost code, a pinned drawing-revision or document reference) and implements **no financial derivation and changes no financial figure**. Numbered **per project** (`RFI-0001` in every project), raised by a named user (a snapshot of the creator's own profile name), assigned to a company **Contact**, with a due date. Lifecycle `draft → open → answered → closed`, plus `cancelled` from draft/open only — **forward-only, no reopen**: once raised, the question and its reference are frozen; once answered, the answer is final; an unsatisfactory answer is closed with a note and a new RFI raised; an answered RFI cannot be cancelled. Overdue, days open and **response time** (between the authored raised and answer dates) are derived at read time and never stored. A drawing reference names the **exact revision** the question was asked against — issuing a newer revision never moves it — and Firestore rules verify the referenced drawing, revision or document exists. Reads and writes: `company_admin`, `project_manager`, `qs`; subcontractor and client access **denied**. **⚠️ A record, not a channel** — nothing is emailed and nobody is notified; answers are transcribed from the correspondence. **⚠️ Financially inert** — raising, answering or closing an RFI changes no budget, commitment, forecast, margin, cash-flow or variation figure. *Not included:* email, notifications, an external portal, attachments, markup, cost/time impact, EOT, variation generation, answer versioning, reopen, threads, distribution lists, submittals, transmittals, approval chains. See ADR-33.
- **Dashboard** — *Implemented (Premium Dashboard v2, ADR-45, over analytics v1, ADR-42).* Company context, Active/Total project counts and a **projects-by-status** bar (counts only — shown as "—"/Unavailable until the project list loads, never a false 0), and the project register (Headline Budget, reported progress, currency; a table on desktop, cards on mobile) — no money summed across projects. Financial roles also get a **Project Snapshot** for **one selected project** (`?project=<id>`): headline KPIs for Approved Budget, Forecast Final Cost with Variance to Budget, Forecast Gross Profit + Margin % (with movement against the original plan when an original budget is set), and Remaining Payable (gross); Actual, Remaining Committed and Uncommitted Cost to Complete stay visible; and a **Budget, cost & revenue** chart (Current Contract Sum, Approved Budget and Forecast Final Cost by composition, on one scale) — every figure the existing Forecast / Commercial / Supplier Payments derivation, in that project's currency. Beside it, a **Programme** panel shows the selected project's activity counts from the Timeline (status donut, completed, overdue, due in 14 days, milestones remaining) — manually maintained, and never presented as % complete, earned value or schedule performance. *Not included:* portfolio money, cash-flow analytics, health indicators/PULSE™, trends over time.

### Tier 4 — Intelligence & assurance (commercially anchored, later)

Reserved proprietary features. They read the commercial spine; they do not introduce a parallel model. PULSE™ (1A + 1B), the SHIELD™ foundation and Quant™ 1A to 1D are live in production (all below); IQ™ remains a placeholder until its planned sprint (see [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md)).

- **Constrapp PULSE™** — *Implemented and live in production (1A ADR-47; 1B ADR-49, released 19 October 2026).* A **deterministic, read-only** project health tab (`/projects/:id/pulse`, financial roles) that evaluates six explainable conditions from the project's own records, each resolving to *loading / unavailable / needs data / finding / no issue found*: Forecast Final Cost exceeds Approved Budget; cost codes not yet forecast; Forecast Gross Profit negative; programme activities overdue; supplier invoices past due with a remaining payable balance; pending supplier and client variation exposure (exposure, not an error). Every figure is an existing Forecast / Commercial / Supplier Payments / Timeline selector output in the project currency, with the source records listed and a link to the workflow that resolves it. An incomplete forecast is reported as *needs data*, never as healthy; an unavailable read is never "no issue". **PULSE™ 1B (ADR-49 — live in production since 19 October 2026)** adds a human **acknowledgement workflow** on those findings: a Company Admin or Project Manager acknowledges a finding with a note and a **required review-by date** (pending variation exposure is "reviewed"), sees who last acknowledged it (You / Another user), withdraws it, and sees the finding return to *Needs attention* when new source records appear or the review date arrives. Acknowledgement is **not** resolution, financial approval or audit certification; the finding, its figures and its evidence stay visible and unchanged, and resolution is derived only when the condition disappears. *Not included (still placeholder/future):* health scores, severity or risk thresholds, trends or history, prediction, margin erosion over time, cash-flow risk, uncertified claim exposure, portfolio health, assignment to a person, notifications or reminders, background monitoring, Dashboard widgets, and any AI.
- **Constrapp SHIELD™** — *Implemented and live in production (foundation, ADR-50, released 19 October 2026).* **Commercial assurance — permanently; never HSEQ or safety management** (the ADR-45 open decision is resolved by ADR-50; the HSEQ anti-goal below stands). A **read-only, project-scoped** tab (`/projects/:id/shield`, financial roles) of exactly five checks over the project's own commercial records, each resolving to *loading / unavailable / needs data / finding / no issue found*: potential duplicate supplier invoices (same supplier, same normalised supplier reference); supplier invoice integrity (stored totals against the invoice's own lines and retention; counted lines with no cost code); purchase order over-invoicing exposure (posted invoiced-to-date above a PO line or the PO subtotal, with approved supplier variations shown as context, never deducted); commitment records requiring reconciliation (cost counted against an uncommitted PO; an open claim on a closed or cancelled PO; more than one open claim per PO; more than one active invoice per claim; and — since ADR-55 — a PO line counted through both a posted direct invoice and an approved claim, with the overlapping amount); and a roll-up of the existing payables exceptions (allocation, credit-note, retention-release, over-release and over-reconciliation) with their originating module. Every figure is an existing selector output in the project currency, every finding lists its source records, and an empty, malformed or unavailable source is reported as exactly that — never as a clean result. A finding is an exception or a condition needing review, **not** evidence of wrongdoing, fraud detection, a compliance certification or a prediction. *Not included (deferred until separately approved):* missing approval evidence, single-actor segregation-of-duties alerts, currency mismatch, duplicate payment alerts, further cost-code or commitment conditions, acknowledgement or any SHIELD persistence, notifications, portfolio views, scores or severity, Dashboard widgets, and any AI.
- **Constrapp IQ™** — *Future.* AI intelligence for schedule, variation, and accountability.
- **Constrapp Quant™** — *Quant 1A, 1B and 1C live in production (ADR-51, ADR-52 — October 2026; ADR-53 — verified 23 October 2026); Quant 1D — manual opening and void deductions (ADR-54) — live in production since 26 October 2026 (rules published first, then Hosting; production-verified). 1D lets the QS trace an opening INSIDE a measured area and deduct it explicitly: the area then reads Gross 30.000 m² · Deductions 5.000 m² · Net 25.000 m², the net is the quantity every total and the BOQ apply consume, a deduction is never a row, a link or a cost code of its own, and holes that leave the outline, overlap or leave no area are refused.* The drawing takeoff module, **manual first**: a project tab (`/projects/:id/quant`, company_admin / project_manager / qs) that renders one page of an existing drawing revision (PDF through a lazily loaded PDF.js, or PNG/JPEG), lets the QS **calibrate** it from one known metric distance (mm or m), and **measure lengths, areas and counts** by placing points. Each measurement is persisted as a traceable record pinned to its drawing revision and page, with a required cost code, a label, canonical page-space geometry and a snapshot of the calibration it was measured under; every quantity (m, m², no.) is **recomputed from geometry × calibration at read time — never stored**. Re-calibrating flags existing lengths and areas *needs review* and keeps their previous quantity explainable; measurements on superseded revisions stay inspectable with a warning and nothing transfers automatically; withdrawn revisions cannot be measured. Edits, re-calibration and voids are revision-guarded transactions (a stale copy is refused, never merged). **The Quant tab writes no BOQ quantity and no financial figure.** **Quant 1B (ADR-52)** connects the two ends explicitly: a measurement may be **linked** to one active BOQ item on the same cost code whose unit accepts its kind (m → `m`/`lm`/metres; m² → `m2`/`sqm`; no. → `no`/`nr`/`each` — no other unit, no conversion); on the BOQ tab an item with linked measurements offers **Quant (n)**, a review dialog listing every linked measurement with its drawing, revision status and quantity, every **blocker** (needs review, superseded or withdrawn revision, uncalibrated page, cost-code or unit mismatch, unreadable record — any one blocks the whole apply), the eligible **total** (summed unrounded, rounded once to three decimals), the existing → proposed quantity and, for a priced item, the existing → proposed amount at the unchanged rate (an unpriced item shows no amount at all); a person then **confirms** and the one item's quantity is applied through a version-guarded write that refuses a stale copy, records the latest application (`quantApplied`), and never touches a budget, forecast, order, claim, invoice, cash-flow or margin figure. *Not included:* automatic syncing, BOQ item creation from measurements, revision reconciliation automation, bulk apply, unit conversion, an audit history, and any **AI-assisted recognition** (still deferred). **Quant 1C-A (ADR-53)** completes the manual workflow's correctness and interaction without touching the data model: an area whose outline crosses or collapses is **refused** at save and, if already stored, reads *cannot be derived* with the reason and is **blocked from BOQ application**; unsaved drafts and geometry edits are **remembered per project in the browser session** and restored on the same sheet, with one explicit confirmation before a drawing, revision, page or tool change discards them and the browser's own prompt before the tab closes; geometry editing gains **insert a point on an edge, remove a selected point, add or remove count markers, undo** and cancel; the **Select** tool selects a measurement by tapping it on the sheet and offers its Details / Geometry / Void; **Ctrl/⌘-wheel and pinch zoom about the pointer, drag-to-pan**; vertex targets are 44 px; and the calibration, details and void dialogs gain the same dialog semantics as the BOQ apply dialog. **Quant 1C-B** finishes the manual workflow with a **Project summary** view on the same tab: per-kind totals (metres, square metres and counts never added together) of current, valid measurements, historical sheets totalled apart, grouping by cost code and by drawing → revision → page, one **attention list** (not linked, needs review, not calibrated, cannot be derived, missing or void or mismatched BOQ item, missing source), the measured date where the record carries one, and **Open** on every row — which selects the exact drawing, revision and page and highlights the measurement, or explains why a voided, missing or withdrawn source cannot be opened; the BOQ tab's apply dialog gains **Open in Quant** per source measurement through the same mechanism. Read-only, no new collection, no stored total.

## What Constrapp Is Not

Constrapp is a commercial-control system, **not a Dashpivot-style form-first platform**. The following are deliberate anti-goals, not backlog items — building them requires an approved strategy change:

- Generic no-code form builders
- Large HSEQ template libraries
- Generic field reporting
- Payroll or broad workforce management
- Fleet or equipment management
- Broad enterprise integrations before product-market fit

Field capability is always in service of a commercial outcome (see the lifecycle above); a feature that cannot state its commercial input or output does not belong in scope.
